A kitchen with no dining room

Noon & Co runs as a virtual restaurant — every dish is cooked to order and sent out for pickup or delivery, nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting for a table that never fills.

Started in Abuja, now serving Lagos too, with a menu built around the meals people actually reorder: jollof, native rice, stir-fried noodles, and combos big enough for the whole table.
